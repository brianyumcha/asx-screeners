Sectors
Materials (XMJ +1.82%) bounced after last week's 12.3% fall, and Health Care (XHJ +0.24%) was the only other gainer. Financials (XFJ −1.20%), A-REITs (XPJ −1.01%) and Discretionary (XDJ −1.00%) were the weakest.
Stocks
BHP +2.2% on plans to double South Australian copper output by 2040. SGM (Sims) +9.6% on split broker calls. ALQ (ALS) −12% despite a jump in first-half profit. CBA −2.22%, WBC −1.89% and ANZ −1.66% led the banks lower.

The ASX sat out the US futures bounce. The banks dragged the index lower, while miners joined in as gold futures rebounded about 3%.
