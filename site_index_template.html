📊 Screeners

Higher-High
Flags ASX stocks breaking out to a new swing high — a close crossing back above the last confirmed pivot high, by sector, across the full ASX universe.
 🚀
Momentum
ASX stocks with a confirmed uptrend and healthy RSI — not already overbought. Sorted by relative volume.
 📈
Pre-Breakout (OBV)
ASX stocks quietly building strength before a breakout — not stocks already breaking out.
 ↩️
Pullback (Zag Zone)
ASX stocks pulling back into the 38.2%-61.8% retracement of their latest swing.
 🌋
Volume Anomaly
ASX stocks trading well above their own normal volume today — no price-direction filter, so quiet accumulation shows up before any pattern is visible.
