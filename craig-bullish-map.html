Where each miner sits on the Lassonde Curve

The classic exploration-to-production value curve: a speculative re-rate on early discovery, a rough "valley of death" through feasibility and financing while the market waits for economics to prove up, then a re-rate again once cash flow actually starts. 42 of the 54 (Materials + Energy resource names — biotech/industrials/IT/consumer don't fit this curve) placed at their current project stage.

This is my read of the project stage from Craig's own calls, not a technical or financial audit — several of these are genuinely hard judgment calls (a company can straddle two stages at once), and I haven't cross-checked every one against the company's actual technical reports. Treat placement as directionally useful, not precise.
