Reversal (RSI)

Classic RSI divergence across the ASX universe, daily and weekly: price prints a new swing extreme that RSI doesn't confirm - an early, not-yet-triggered reversal signal, same mechanics Jamie Bell describes picking up. A pivot pair only qualifies if it's at least 5 bars apart, the 2nd pivot formed within the last 8 bars, and price hasn't since closed back past that pivot's level (which would invalidate it). No minimum RSI-gap filter - a weak divergence still shows, just with a small gap number instead of being hidden.
